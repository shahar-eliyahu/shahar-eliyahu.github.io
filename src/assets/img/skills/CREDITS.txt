# Technology logos

Used by the Skills section (mapping: `src/_data/skillLogos.json`).

- **devicon** 2.18.0, https://github.com/devicons/devicon (MIT, licence below):
  pytorch, tensorflow, scikitlearn, huggingface, python, java, kotlin, c, cplusplus, opencv, pandas, numpy, apachespark, apachekafka, android, git, androidstudio, visualstudio, azure, googlecloud, firebase, claude, githubcopilot.
- **Simple Icons** 16.36.0, https://simpleicons.org (CC0 1.0), filled with the brand colour:
  jetpackcompose, github, linux, cursor, mediapipe, yolo, nvidia.

Black or navy parts that would disappear on the dark tags (scikit-learn text, pandas bars,
Kafka, GitHub, Cursor, YOLO) use the light colour #E2E8F0, as on those brands' dark-background
versions. All logos are trademarks of their respective owners and are shown only to name the
technology.
